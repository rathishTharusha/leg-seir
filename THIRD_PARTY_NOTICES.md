# Rights and attribution

The MIT license applies to original team software. It does not grant rights over government source reports, population tables, geography/climate inputs, third-party research papers or borrowed code. The manuscript and dataset require their own applicable permissions.

No third-party paper PDFs, lecturer materials or bulk raw source archives are included. Historical team-authored reproduction wrappers download pinned upstream code from its original repository. Their scope records retain attribution and distinguish original-task reruns from adaptations; upstream licenses govern downloaded implementations. Research citations alone are not software licenses.

Dataset provider attribution, file-specific terms, redistribution status and retrieval provenance belong in the dataset card and source manifest. Do not label the dataset MIT because the software uses MIT.
